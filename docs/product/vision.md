# CaptureOS vision

CaptureOS will become a local-first intelligent operating system for professional camera media. Its promise is: **your entire shoot, alive, understood, protected.**

The system will understand a complete shoot across stills, RAWs, video, audio, devices, storage volumes, people, moments, backups, edits, and exports. Its core relationship model is called CaptureGraph.

## Product stance

CaptureOS is not a Lightroom, Premiere, Dropbox, delivery gallery, chatbot, normal file manager, or dashboard. It is a local intelligence layer that sits alongside creators' chosen tools and storage.

Original media remains on user-controlled SD/CFexpress cards, SSDs, HDDs, internal disks, NAS volumes, and folders. Offline operation is fundamental. A future optional cloud capability must not become a core requirement.

## Future direction

Potential modules include CaptureGraph, Studio Brain, Magic Ingest, Media GPS, Archive Guardian, CaptureGuardian, Shoot Universe, Magic Search, People and Moment Brain, coverage and continuity intelligence, media DNA, edit-ready handoff, provenance, integrations, and an optional companion app. This vision is a direction, not a statement that these capabilities exist today.

## Current reality through Milestone 6

Phase 0 established the safe core: strong domain types, graph relationships, a local SQLite repository, migration discipline, adapter boundaries, job records, and a synthetic Golden Wedding fixture. Milestone 1 added read-only, additive Index Mode; Milestone 2 added verified, non-destructive local ingest and CaptureGuardian evidence; Milestone 3 added the local visual grid, metadata, viewer, and CaptureOS-managed preview cache.

Milestone 4 adds the first real local intelligence layer for eligible photos: deterministic duplicate/related-frame evidence, bounded similarity and burst grouping, technical sharpness/blur/exposure signals, optional local face/eye technical evidence, and conservative recommendations with visible reasons and confidence. Milestone 5 adds human-controlled local culling decisions, ratings, notes, and similarity-set representatives without mutating source media.

Milestone 6 adds current-project Magic Search: optional locally installed image/text embeddings plus deterministic filters for supported still photographs. It is a local retrieval surface, not a chatbot or creative judge. It cannot identify people, infer demographics, perform cross-project person search, understand events, analyze video/audio, alter human decisions, or contact a cloud service.

The later modules named above remain product direction only. They are not enabled by the M6 architecture without explicit future approval, privacy design, model/license review, and user-visible controls.
